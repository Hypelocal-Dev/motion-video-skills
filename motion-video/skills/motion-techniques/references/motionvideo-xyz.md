# MotionVideo (motionvideo.xyz)

[MotionVideo](https://motionvideo.xyz) has two parts that help turn a rough idea into
something concrete:

- **A gallery** of motion videos made with AI coding agents, each shown with its prompt.
  Entries live at `motionvideo.xyz/videos/<creator>-<id>`. Each video belongs to its
  creator. Open the entry to see it, and credit the creator if you build on it.
- **Moves**, a catalog of named motion-design techniques at
  `motionvideo.xyz/moves/<category>/<move>`. Each move page says what it looks like and when
  it works. The copy-ready prompts are behind a paid plan.

**Terms.** Individual prompts may be used in your own and client work. Copying the move or
template prompts in bulk, or redistributing them, isn't allowed. Gallery videos stay with
their creators. So link to entries and describe techniques in your own words. Never paste
their prompts into this repo.

## Gallery categories

`motionvideo.xyz/category/<slug>`: Product UI, Phone, Charts, Diagrams, Kinetic type,
Shapes, Particles, Characters, Photos, Music, Code.
