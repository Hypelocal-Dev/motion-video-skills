# Prompt Motion entries worth studying

[Prompt Motion](https://prompt-motion.com) collects motion videos made with Claude and
the prompts behind them. Each video and prompt belongs to its creator. Open the entry
to read the original, and credit the creator if you build on it.

| Entry | Creator | Why it's useful |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [White ball UI motion loop](https://prompt-motion.com/rossaxbt-94583b) | @rossaxbt | One continuous world with a camera, a single element that leads every transition, and a seamless loop |
| [Shape morphing through UI states](https://prompt-motion.com/twoclipping-5cba86) | @twoclipping | One shape morphing through UI states with real cursor input, springs and a first-frame-equals-last-frame loop |
| [Morphing UI states loop](https://prompt-motion.com/demonugc-4c5753) | @demonugc | A variant of the morphing approach with beat-locked timing |
| [Orange dot motion system](https://prompt-motion.com/ultimaxbt-bbebf5) | @ultimaxbt | A connecting element that travels through every scene and returns on the end card |
| [SaaS launch video](https://prompt-motion.com/aschapmann-131210) | @aschapmann | Story structure for a product film: why it matters, the pain, the fix, what you get |
| [Paper-style product launch film](https://prompt-motion.com/ik-builds-b8bdcf) | @ik_builds | A HyperFrames and GSAP prompt with strong rules on copy, sound and seek-safe animation |
| [Supademo launch video](https://prompt-motion.com/jhylee95-e1152f) | @jhylee95 | Scoring cuts to the beat of the music |

Browse by category on the site. **Product UI**, **Phone** and **Kinetic type** are the
closest to this plugin's templates.
