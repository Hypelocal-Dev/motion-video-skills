# Finding moments

A good clip makes sense to someone who never saw the long video, and gives them a
reason to watch to the end.

## Length

- 15-45 seconds for most feeds. Up to 60 seconds for a complete how-to step.
- Start on the first word of the idea, not the setup before it. End on the payoff
  line, not the transition after it.

## What makes a moment

For tutorials and demos:

- **A complete mini-lesson:** one step, its reason and its result ("Make the AI audit
  its own work").
- **A gotcha and its fix:** the mistake people make and what to do instead.
- **A surprising result:** a number, a before and after, the tool doing something hard.
- **The thesis:** the one line that sums up the method.
- **A strong claim with proof on screen.**

For talk (podcasts, interviews, talking heads):

- **Punchlines and reactions:** "wait", "no way", laughter, a swear.
- **Reversals:** a question with an unexpected answer.
- **Self-roasts and one-liners:** short sentences that stand alone.
- **Quick back-and-forth:** several short alternating lines.
- **Energy peaks:** louder, faster stretches.

## Checks before you pick it

- Does it need context from earlier? If yes, either start earlier or skip it.
- Does the title say the hook in plain words? 2-7 words, no clickbait the clip can't
  pay off.
- Is the visual doing work? For screen recordings, the thing being talked about should
  be on screen during the clip.
- Is anything private on screen at any point in the clip? (Run the frame check.)
